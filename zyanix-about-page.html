About Zyanix

We're a Team of Builders, Not Just Coders

Zyanix started with a simple idea: great software should be built by people who care about the outcome as much as the client does. Eight years later, that's still how we work.

Our Story

From a Two-Person Studio to a Global Team

Zyanix began in 2018 as a two-person freelance studio building websites for local businesses. As word spread about the quality of our work, we grew into a full-service digital partner trusted by startups and enterprises across 15+ countries.

Today, our team spans design, engineering, product and DevOps — but the mindset hasn't changed. We still treat every project like it's the only one that matters.
